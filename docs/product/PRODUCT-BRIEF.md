# Product Brief

## 1. Problem
What painful, frequent, or valuable problem are we solving?

## 2. Target users
Who experiences the problem? Segment primary vs secondary users.

## 3. Current alternatives
How do users solve this today? Include manual work and “do nothing.”

## 4. Value proposition
What materially improves if this product exists?

## 5. Desired outcomes
List user and business outcomes, not features.

## 6. Success metrics
Define measurable leading and lagging indicators.

## 7. Constraints
Budget, timeline, platforms, regulations, integrations, team skills, latency, reliability, geography, etc.

## 8. Assumptions
List assumptions that could invalidate the plan. Mark how each will be tested.

## 9. Risks
Product, technical, operational, security, legal/compliance, dependency, and adoption risks.

## 10. Non-goals
Explicitly state what this project will not solve in the current horizon.

## 11. Open questions
Track unresolved questions with owner and due date when appropriate.
