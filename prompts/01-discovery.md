# Prompt: Product Discovery

Use this before architecture or implementation.

```text
Act as a product discovery partner, not an implementation agent.

Goal: turn the idea below into a testable product brief without prematurely choosing a stack or architecture.

Idea:
<PASTE IDEA>

Produce:
1. problem statement;
2. primary and secondary users;
3. jobs-to-be-done / desired outcomes;
4. existing alternatives;
5. value proposition;
6. assumptions ranked by risk;
7. constraints;
8. explicit non-goals;
9. success metrics;
10. major product/technical/operational/security risks;
11. unanswered questions;
12. recommended discovery experiments before major implementation.

Separate facts supplied by me from assumptions you introduce. Do not invent market evidence.
```
